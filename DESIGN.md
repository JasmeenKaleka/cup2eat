# CUP2EAT design and content audit
Brief: Build a cup-centered food commercial for cafes, events and wholesale buyers using the existing Instagram archive. No previous application exists.
Concepts (originality / fit / feasibility / portability):
- The last sip is the first bite: a cup travels from beverage to biscuit. 4/5/5/1. Chosen because the edible vessel is the whole brand proposition.
- Biscuit architecture: macro texture becomes the layout grid. 4/4/3/2.
- Chai social ritual: a table of shared cups and event moments. 3/5/4/2.
Concept card: The last sip is the first bite. Metaphor: three acts of a drink that becomes food. Signature: large authentic cup on a warm illuminated stage, followed by a sticky three-chapter narrative. Emotion: appetite and curiosity. Motion verb: pour and settle. Screenshot: oversized green EAT YOUR DRINK beside a floating biscuit cup.
System: oat #f4efdf, forest #173f2e, caramel #b78144, chocolate #39251c, burgundy #743b3c. Georgia display and Arial UI are system fonts, avoiding external font loading. Twelve-column conceptual grid, 5vw margins, 1px rules, pill actions and large circular product stage. GSAP power3 easing, 0.5�1.2s, one scoped context, reduced motion static fallback. No WebGL: photo depth preserves the actual product.
Composition: poster hero / sticky three-act story / asymmetric interactive collection / typographic feature ribbon / certificate index / dark business enquiry finale.
Genericness check: no repeated card grid; product selectors are an editorial list and one large photo stage.
Sources: cup2eat downloaded Instagram captions, images and compressed JSON metadata. asset-index.txt maps original filenames. Existing profile photo is the unchanged logo.
